What affects the cost of a website?

When you compare quotes, these factors explain most price differences. Ask about each before you decide.

Size

Number of pages

A single page with the essentials takes less work than a website with a page for every service.

Design

Custom or template

How closely the design fits your brand and how many sections are customized.

Languages

One or more languages

Each language adds copy to prepare, review, and maintain.

Features

Beyond information

Booking tools, an online store, or a CRM or ERP connection require work beyond the design.

Revisions

Rounds of changes

How many rounds of design and content changes are included before launch.

After launch

Ongoing costs

Domain, hosting, and maintenance. Always ask whether they're included or paid separately.

Pricing questions

Are there monthly fees or a minimum commitment?

No. It's a one-time payment, with no required recurring fees or minimum commitment. The website is yours once payment is complete.

How does payment work?

Essential is paid in full when you book. Business and Professional are 50% upfront and 50% before launch.

Are the domain and hosting included?

They're not included in the price. We help you set them up or use what you already have.

When does the delivery timeline start?

Timelines are estimates and start when we receive the content we need. Delays in materials or approvals can extend them.

What if I need more revisions or features?

Additional revisions cost extra. New features, major structural changes, and CRM or ERP integrations are quoted separately.
