Cookies

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Analytics and advertising cookies

If we use analytics or advertising cookies, they are installed only when you accept them in the cookie notice. You can accept, reject, or change your choice at any time using Cookie settings.

Technical storage

Our hosting may log your IP address and the requested page to serve the page and protect the service. This is not used to build a profile.

If you choose light or dark mode, your browser saves your theme preference in localStorage, under the key autom8-theme, for your next visit. Your cookie choice is saved in localStorage under autom8-consent for 13 months. Neither choice is ever sent to a server. You can clear them in your browser settings.

The autom8-lang language cookie lasts for 12 months. It is set only when you pick ES or EN in the language switcher, so we can show the site in that language next time. This cookie is sent only to our site for that purpose.

If we add a new service, we update this page and ask for your consent before activating it. When you withdraw consent, we delete that service's cookies on this site.
